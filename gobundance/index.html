Why GoBundance, why now

Two gatherings full of the people GoBundance is built for.

You’ve already been in the room

David and his crew joined us at Iceland Eclipse 2026: 4,200 people from 75 countries beneath totality. These are the next two chapters of that story.

Two great rivers, one year

Coherence gathers on the Colorado River in Austin, half an hour from GoBundance’s Base Camp in Dripping Springs, and closes on World Water Day. Egypt follows the Nile to the longest totality on land for the next 87 years. Water is the thread between them.

A front door to who’s next

Coherence and Egypt welcome founders, healers, creatives, partners and families: the people GoBundance Women, Fambundance and Emerge exist to reach. A partner presence puts GoBundance in front of them in person.

Contribution you can see

Part of every dollar holds seats for artists, scientists and changemakers who couldn’t otherwise be there, named in GoBundance’s honor and in step with its goal of touching 10 million lives by 2028.

Three days of music, ceremony and collective action on the Colorado River in Austin. Saturday on the Spring Equinox looks at the most optimistic next 10 to 15 years. Sunday under the Full Moon is about optimizing mind, body, heart and spirit. Monday, World Water Day, explores the technology and consciousness of water, a thread that runs straight to the Nile in August. Six headline acts play at night.

Included · $50,000 valuePresenting Sponsorship

  • “GoBundance presents Coherence”, category exclusive, named at the launch announcement and in every moment through the recap film
  • Media: title partner on the press release and press kit, logo on all creative, a dedicated social campaign, and a full page in the festival guide and app
  • On site: major logo at the gate and the main stage, named on every wristband, app push notifications, and the shuttle program
  • Hospitality: 8 VIP and 20 GA passes, 2 RV Village pads, seats at the artist and speaker dinner, and 4 all-areas production passes
  • Activation: a custom GoBundance space, product seeding in the VIP lounge, and the keynote introduction on one themed day

GoBundance’s March Base Camp in Dripping Springs runs March 21 to 23, alongside Coherence. We’d love to build a GoBundance moment into Sunday’s program so new members can join the gathering together.

The investment$250,000 of the $500,000 round

  • Preferred membership interest. Investors receive 80% of profits until their capital is returned plus a competitive hurdle return, then keep 20% of profits.
  • GoBundance’s share is pro rata: 40% of profits until $250,000 plus the hurdle is returned, then 10% ongoing.
  • Use of funds: the round covers most of the $528K of deposits and pre-event costs due before ticket sales carry the load.

A seven-night Nile journey from Aswan to Luxor and back, the Eclipse Gathering on Luxor’s West Bank, and three days at the Pyramids closing with the 8/8 Lion’s Gate. Artists, scientists, astronauts and founders travel together, with temple visits at dawn and music after totality.

Included · $100,000 valueAnchor Partnership

  • Named Anchor Partner of Egypt Eclipse across every chapter of the journey, in the press release and press kit
  • A signature experience carrying GoBundance’s name, chosen together: the Nile Voyage, Dawn Balloons over the Valley of the Kings, or the Lion’s Gate at Giza
  • Content: credits in the official film and recap, features across the Eclipse community’s social and newsletters, and full photo and video coverage delivered after
  • Presence: a bespoke activation at the Eclipse Gathering, welcome-kit seeding, and a private hosted evening for GoBundance guests

The Celebrity is our most curated boat: a hand-picked circle of artists, scientists and founders on the seven-night journey from Aswan to Luxor and back. Every place includes the Nile journey, temple visits with Egyptologists, the programming on board and the Eclipse Gathering in Luxor.

  • Presence: patron-only dinners, sunrise walks and time with the founders and featured co-creators, plus the three-day Giza Activation for the suite
  • Voice: a seat at the design table for Egypt and the gatherings that follow, and a session or ritual that carries GoBundance’s name and intention
  • Legacy: recognition as an Anchor Patron of the Eclipse Trilogy, and sponsored seats held in GoBundance’s name for artists, scientists and changemakers
  • For members: priority access for GoBundance members and families to join the journey, by arrangement

Patronage underwrites the artists we commission, the scientists we host, the production that shapes the experience, and seats for people who couldn’t otherwise be in the room.
