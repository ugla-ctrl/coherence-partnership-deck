01 · Opportunity
Partnership Overview
Why Partner

As AI makes goods and services cheaper, spending moves to what can't be automated: being there.

Three worlds, one place

Ceremony and consciousness, AI and technology, music and celebration. Austin is known for each one on its own, never for all three together.

New money, seeking meaning

Austin startups raised a record $7.19B in 2025, led by AI. The people building the post-AI economy live here, and they buy experiences, not things.

Spending shifts to wellbeing

The global wellness economy grew 7.9% in 2024 and is forecast to reach $9.8T by 2029. Wellness travel is outpacing travel overall.

Attention without the noise

During SXSW, brands compete for every square foot downtown. On the river, your activation is one of a few, in front of guests who stay for three days.

Sources: Crunchbase · Global Wellness Institute · SXSW
